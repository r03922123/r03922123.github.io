On a self-hosted MusicGen pipeline (audiocraft, PyTorch, batched GPU generation), I scored the partial candidates against the prompt with CLAP at the 10 s and 20 s marks of a 30 s clip and dropped the weaker half each time (8 → 4 → 2), with no noticeable drop in the final CLAP score or in listening.   • Fixing product names in live Japanese captions. For an automotive client, a post-correction layer on Azure speech-to-text recovered ~75% of the product terms known to be mis-transcribed: Janome tokenization, katakana normalization and RapidFuzz matching over a 1–3 morpheme window. It adds ~1 ms inside a sub-50 ms streaming budget on client-owned hardware.
  • An automated first pass for text-to-music evaluation. MAEST style tags, taken chunk by chunk, were checked against the prompt by an LLM judge alongside a CLAP score. Clear passes and clear rejects were decided automatically, and only the borderline ~40% of clips went to a human, removing ~60% of manual listening time.
